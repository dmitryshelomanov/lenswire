## Short description (≤80 chars)

Local HTTP(S) inspector — VPN MITM, decrypt, mock & rewrite on device.

## Full description

Lenswire is a native HTTP(S) inspector for Android. Capture device traffic with a local VPN MITM, decrypt HTTPS when the Lenswire CA is installed, inspect requests and responses, and mock or rewrite traffic — all on device.

Device VPN capture
Android VpnService routes traffic through a local MITM proxy. No desktop required.

HTTPS decryption
Install the Lenswire CA, enable HTTPS decryption, and read requests and responses in plain text.

Domain & client filters
Group by host, filter by method, status, client (OkHttp and more), and search paths fast.

Request & response detail
Headers, JSON tree/raw body preview, timing, Copy as cURL, and Share HAR.

Mock & rewrite overrides
Mock responses without hitting the server, or rewrite request payloads before they leave the device.

On-device privacy
No remote proxy servers — captures stay on your phone.
